Scope: /tienda (public storefront, Persuade) plus / (selector) and /admin (Operate, restrained). Inherits the identity from the source PDF (sand/cream, olive, petrol, serif display).

Audience/job: shoppers browse, add to cart and confirm a demo order. Owner edits the catalog and settings.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The online store is the shop window of a neighborhood shop. It refuses the generic hero-plus-card-grid of templates: the hero is a shop window (vidriera) with a shelf, objects and a hanging price tag, and the catalog is the shop shelf.
OWN-WORLD: Sand #E8DFD0 and paper #F7F3EC grounds, olive #58633F for actions, petrol #22414F filling whole bands (announcement, close, checkout summary), espresso ink #2F2924. Gelasio serif for display (a Georgia-metric face, continuing the PDF), Hanken Grotesk for UI. Kraft-paper tags with a punched hole and string for prices and badges. Hairlines 1px in warm sand, radii 6px.
STORY: The visitor sees it is a real shop in Isidro Casanova, browses, filters, adds (feedback on the button and on the cart), opens the cart drawer, sees the free-shipping progress and installments, and confirms a demo order.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Petrol announcement bar; header with a serif wordmark, nav and an olive cart button. Hero: left, a 5–6rem serif headline plus copy and an "Explorar la tienda" CTA; right, a framed shop window with two shelves, authored objects and a real bag photo, a kraft tag with the address.
FORM: Shop window / shelf (1st on my list). seed: none (pinned)  "explotar la identidad que ya generó" (user) + the source PDF.
Signature interaction: adding to cart makes the product's tag fly toward the cart button, and the count bumps. Motion: ease-out-quint, 180–420ms, prefers-reduced-motion respected.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
